VVZ ERP Suite for garages and auto repair.

While a mechanic is working, a call can go unanswered. Someone has to call back, find the vehicle, remember the promised diagnosis, check parts and reconstruct earlier messages. With paper, Excel and details kept in people’s heads, important information is easily scattered. The problem is not the phone, Excel or messaging itself: the process depends too much on human memory.

The real problem: scattered information

A customer is waiting for a callback, a vehicle stays at the garage, the diagnosis changes and a part must be ordered. The team needs to see who contacted the garage, why, what has already happened and what comes next.

Before

Missed calls, messages, paper notes, Excel tables and memory are mixed together. The customer calls again just to ask what is happening with the vehicle.

After

Each request is recorded with the customer, vehicle, required work, responsible person, status and preserved history.

Typical requests and tasks in a garage

Diagnostics

Reported symptom, vehicle, priority and information for the workshop to check.

Repairs and parts

The required work is recorded together with the parts to plan for. The team can see what needs checking, what is available, what must be ordered and what is still blocking the repair.

Workshop follow-up

Inspection, maintenance, tyres or another task with an owner and useful notes.

A simple path from request to history

Customer request

The customer describes the problem or required work.

Vehicle and problem

The vehicle, reason for the visit and useful details are recorded together.

Diagnosis and work

The team clarifies the diagnosis, required work and parts to identify.

New

A new request appears in the workshop and can be assigned to the person who should act.

In progress

When someone takes it on, it moves into progress and important information can be added as the work continues.

Done and history

Once the work is finished, the status changes, but all information remains available in the history.

Fewer calls just to ask “what is happening with my car?”

Depending on the chosen configuration, the customer can receive a confirmation when the request is recorded, followed by an update when the status changes. The team stays focused on the work instead of answering the same question several times.

The garage keeps familiar working habits

Excel can remain part of the workflow

Existing tables can continue to be useful. The journal can use familiar columns such as customer, vehicle, date, work, status, responsible person, notes and history.

An export that works in Excel

Useful data can be exported as a CSV file compatible with Excel, with a structure adapted to the information you actually use.

Information remains accessible where the team works

The local tool

The garage can use its local work tool to follow requests, vehicles, tasks and statuses.

The server

The server is involved only where needed, for example to receive a request from the website or synchronize certain information.

Telegram

Telegram can simply act as an alert when a new request arrives. The main follow-up remains in the tool.

Visible responsibility

Each task can show who is acting, what is waiting for a decision and what has already been completed.

Adapted to the workshop

VVZ Studio adapts the journal, steps and roles to the existing way of working instead of imposing a rigid product or replacing everything.

Frequently asked questions

Is this workflow mandatory?

No. The request, vehicle, diagnosis, parts and repair path is a starting point and can be adapted to the workshop.

Does Telegram replace the journal?

No. Telegram can alert the team; the record, responsible person, status and history stay in the chosen tool.

Do we have to abandon Excel?

No. Useful columns can be kept and data can be exported as a CSV file compatible with Excel.
