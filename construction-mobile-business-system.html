VVZ ERP Suite for construction companies and mobile teams

When several sites or jobs are moving forward at once, information changes quickly: a customer changes a request, a team is waiting for an instruction, a material must be planned, an address or time changes and someone needs to know the latest correct information. If everything stays between calls, messages, paper, Excel and everyone’s memory, the team loses time searching for what was decided.

The real problem: the latest information is not always in the right place

On a site, a small change can have immediate consequences. The team on site needs to know what is planned, what changed, what is missing and who must make the next decision. When this information is scattered, the manager has to call back, search messages or check several documents before answering.

Before

The address is in the schedule, a customer change in a message, materials to prepare in a note and the latest instruction sometimes remains only in the memory of the person who took the call.

After

Each request or job can bring together the customer, site, address, planned work, useful information, responsible person, status and history.

Typical situations for a construction company

New site or job

The customer, address, requested work, planned date and necessary information are recorded with the request.

Team and responsible person

The request can show who should intervene, who follows the site and who should answer when a decision is needed.

Preparation and materials

Items to prepare or necessary materials can be noted with the job so the team can see what must be checked before starting.

Change during the job

If the customer changes a request, a date moves or a new action becomes necessary, the information can be added to the follow-up instead of remaining only in a call or conversation.

A simple path from request to completed work

Customer request

The customer describes the work, location and main constraints.

Site and address

The address, related site and useful information are grouped in the same request.

Work to complete

The team sees what is planned, what must be prepared and what still needs confirmation.

Assigned

The request can be entrusted to the person or team that should intervene.

In progress

The responsible person can follow progress and add important information as the work continues.

Change or decision

When a change occurs, it can be recorded with the request so the team finds the latest useful information.

Done and history

Once the work is complete, the status changes but the information, decisions and completed actions remain available in the history.

Fewer calls just to ask “how is the site progressing?”

Depending on the chosen configuration, the customer can receive a confirmation or an update when certain steps change. This avoids some follow-up calls and lets the team stay focused on the work.

The company keeps familiar working habits

Excel can remain part of the workflow

If you already use tables to track sites, teams, dates or tasks, that organization can remain a reference. The journal can keep the information that is genuinely useful to your activity.

An export that works in Excel

Useful data can be exported as a CSV file compatible with Excel, with a structure adapted to your working columns.

Information remains accessible where the team works

The local tool

The company can use a local tool to find requests, sites, addresses, tasks, responsible people, statuses and notes.

The server

The server is involved only where needed, for example to receive a request from the website or synchronize certain information.

Telegram

Telegram can simply notify authorized people when a new request arrives or an important change needs to be seen quickly. The main follow-up remains in the tool.

Visible responsibility

Each request can show who should intervene, who is waiting for a decision and what action still needs to be completed.

Adapted to your company

VVZ Studio adapts information, steps and roles to the way you work instead of imposing a rigid way of operating.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current schedule?

No. If your current schedule works well, it can remain in place. The goal is mainly to connect information that is currently lost between requests, sites, messages, teams and follow-up.

Do we need to manage the whole site in VVZ ERP Suite?

No. The scope depends on your need. The system can simply follow useful requests, tasks and changes without replacing all your construction-management tools.

Do we have to abandon Excel?

No. Existing tables can remain a reference and useful data can be exported as a CSV compatible with Excel.
