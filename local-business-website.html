The real problem: the business depends too much on one person’s memory

In a small business, one person may answer customers, organize work, pass on information and check what remains to be done. While the volume is low, this works. Once several requests arrive at the same time, it becomes easy to forget a callback, a promise or the next action.

Before

One request is in a call, another in a message, a task in Excel and a callback simply remains in the memory of the person who spoke with the customer.

After

Each request can bring together the customer, their need, responsible person, next action, status and history.

Typical situations in a local business

New request

A customer asks for a service, price, availability or information, and the request is recorded instead of remaining only in the channel where it arrived.

Reply to prepare

A request needing a callback, quote or check can remain visible with the next action to take.

Task to assign

When another person needs to act, the request can clearly show who is responsible.

Request waiting

If a customer reply, decision, availability or other information is still needed, the status can show why the request has not moved forward.

A simple path from request to completed work

Request received

The customer’s need is recorded with useful information.

To be handled

The request remains visible while a reply or action is still needed.

Assigned

It can be given to the person who must reply or complete the work.

In progress

The team sees that the request is being handled and can add important information as work progresses.

Waiting

If something blocks the next step, the status can clearly show what is still expected.

Completed and history

Once the work is complete, the status changes but the information and completed actions remain available in the history.

Fewer customers forced to call back to ask about their request

Depending on the chosen configuration, the customer can receive confirmation when the request is recorded or information when its status changes. The team avoids some repetitive exchanges without losing control of the customer relationship.

The business keeps its familiar points of reference

Your current tools can stay in place

If your phone, messaging, website, calendar or another tool already works well, there is no need to replace everything. VVZ ERP Suite can mainly connect the information and actions that remain scattered today.

Excel can remain part of the workflow

If you already use a table to follow customers, tasks, dates or statuses, it can remain a reference. The journal can include the information genuinely useful to your activity.

An export that works with Excel

Useful data can be exported as a CSV file compatible with Excel, with a structure adapted to your working columns.

Information remains accessible where the team works

The local tool

The business can use a local tool to find requests, tasks, responsible people, statuses, callbacks and notes.

The server

The server is involved only in functions that need it, for example receiving a request from the website or synchronizing some information.

Telegram

Telegram can simply notify authorized people when a new request arrives or when an important action needs to be seen quickly. The main follow-up remains in the tool.

Visible responsibility

Each request can show who should reply, who should act, what is still expected and what has already been done.

Adapted to your business

VVZ Studio adapts information, steps and roles to the way you work instead of imposing a rigid process.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current tools?

No. Tools that work well can stay in place. The goal is mainly to prevent important information from being scattered across channels and depending only on memory.

Is a full-company CRM mandatory?

No. The scope depends on your needs. The solution can remain simple and focus on requests, tasks, responsible people, statuses and useful information.

Do we need to stop using Excel?

No. Existing tables can remain a reference and useful data can be exported as a CSV compatible with Excel.
