VVZ ERP Suite for cleaning companies and home services

When several jobs are scheduled for the same day, one small forgotten detail can disrupt the work: a changed address, an access instruction, a customer moving the time, a team replaced at the last minute or an instruction left in a message. The problem is not the phone, Excel or messaging: useful information is scattered across several places and depends too much on everyone’s memory.

The real problem: every job has its own details

Two cleaning jobs may look similar, but the address, time, access, customer instructions and responsible person may be different. When information stays only in a call or message, the team has to search for it when it is needed.

Before

Addresses are in a table, instructions in a conversation, changes in another message and some information remains in the memory of the person who spoke with the customer.

After

Each request can bring together the customer, address, planned job, useful instructions, responsible person, status and history.

Typical requests for a cleaning company

Address and job

The address, service type, planned date and necessary information are kept with the request.

Customer instructions

Useful details for the job can be noted in the same place so the team does not have to search for an old message before starting.

Team and responsible person

The request can show who should carry out the job and who should handle a change or customer question.

Last-minute change

If the time, address, team or instruction changes, the information can be updated in the request instead of remaining only in a separate conversation.

A simple path from request to completed job

Customer request

The customer requests a job, gives the location, the need and the important information.

Address and instructions

The address and useful instructions are recorded with the request.

Assignment

The request can be assigned to the person or team that should carry out the job.

Scheduled

The planned date and necessary information remain visible before the job.

In progress

The team can see what must be done and note useful information during the work.

Done and history

Once the job is complete, the status changes and the history remains available to find what was planned and completed.

Fewer calls just to ask “is the job still scheduled?”

Depending on the chosen configuration, the customer can receive a confirmation when the request is recorded or when a job is confirmed. This avoids some follow-up calls and repeated exchanges for the team.

The company keeps familiar working habits

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current schedule?

No. If your current organization works well, it can remain in place. The goal is mainly to prevent important information from being separated between the schedule, messages, tables and the team’s memory.

Can last-minute changes be managed?

Yes. A change of address, time, responsible person or instruction can be linked to the request so the team finds the latest useful information in the same place.

Do we have to abandon Excel?

No. Existing tables can remain a reference and useful data can be exported as a CSV compatible with Excel.
