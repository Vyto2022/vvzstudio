VVZ ERP Suite for tyre centres and fitting

When the season begins, calls and requests can arrive almost at the same time: one customer is looking for a precise size, another wants to know whether the tyres are available, a third needs to move an appointment and one set of tyres is still being delivered. If information stays between the phone, messages, paper, Excel and the team’s memory, a size mistake or a forgotten appointment can quickly disrupt the day.

The real problem: everything speeds up at once

During busy periods, the team must answer customers, check sizes, confirm availability, organize orders and fill the fitting schedule. One missing piece of information can mean calling the customer back, searching for a reference or moving an appointment.

Before

Sizes are written in one message, the appointment in another tool, availability has to be checked again and some requests simply remain in the memory of the person who answered the phone.

After

Each request can bring together the customer, vehicle, required sizes, availability, possible order, appointment, responsible person and history.

Typical requests from a tyre centre

Sizes and references

The vehicle, requested sizes and useful information are recorded with the request so the search does not have to start again at every exchange.

Availability and ordering

The team can see whether tyres are available, need to be ordered or are still waiting for supplier confirmation.

Appointments and fitting

The request stays linked to the planned slot, type of work and information needed to prepare the fitting.

Mobile fitting

For a mobile service, the address, vehicle, tyres concerned and planned slot can remain together in the same request.

A simple path from request to fitting

Customer request

The customer asks for a price, availability, appointment or fitting for a precise size.

Vehicle and tyres

The vehicle, sizes and useful information are recorded in the same record.

Availability

The team indicates whether the tyres are available, need ordering or are still awaiting confirmation.

Appointment

Once availability is confirmed, the fitting slot can be linked to the request.

Preparing

The team sees what must be ready before the customer arrives or before the fitter travels.

Fitted and history

Fewer calls just to ask “have my tyres arrived?”

Depending on the chosen configuration, the customer can receive a confirmation when the request is recorded, when the tyres are available or when an appointment is confirmed. This reduces some follow-up calls during the busiest periods.

The centre keeps familiar working habits

Excel can remain part of the workflow

If you already use a table to track customers, sizes, orders or appointments, that organization can remain a reference. The journal can keep the information that is genuinely useful to your activity.

An export that works in Excel

Information remains accessible where the team works

The local tool

The centre can use a local tool to find requests, vehicles, sizes, orders, appointments, statuses and notes.

The server

The server is involved only where needed, for example to receive a request from the website or synchronize certain information.

Telegram

Telegram can simply notify authorized people when a new request arrives or an important action is needed. The main follow-up remains in the tool.

Visible responsibility

Each request can show who must check availability, prepare an order, confirm an appointment or carry out the work.

Adapted to the centre

VVZ Studio adapts information, steps and roles to the way you work instead of imposing a rigid way of operating.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our appointment system?

No. If your current organization works well, it can remain in place. The goal is mainly to connect information that is currently lost between requests, tyres, orders and fitting.

Do we need to manage all stock in VVZ ERP Suite?

No. The scope depends on your need. The system can simply follow availability for a request or connect to a more complete process if necessary.

Do we have to abandon Excel?

No. Existing tables can remain a reference and useful data can be exported as a CSV compatible with Excel.
